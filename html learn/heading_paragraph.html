Bangladesh

Bangladesh is a sovereign and naturally beautiful country located in South Asia. The nation achieved its independence on December 16, 1971, following a heroic nine-month Liberation War against Pakistani forces. Dhaka is the capital and largest city of Bangladesh, renowned for its rich history and vibrant culture.

Bengali (Bangla) is the official state language, spoken proudly by the vast majority of its population. Covering an area of 147,570 square kilometers, it is one of the most densely populated nations, home to over 170 million warm-hearted people.

It is a land of rivers, with mighty waters like the Padma, Meghna, and Jamuna flowing across the country and shaping its landscape. The economy is mainly agricultural, with the majority of the people directly or indirectly engaged in farming. The country boasts incredible natural wonders, including the world's longest natural sandy beach in Cox's Bazar and the largest mangrove forest, the Sundarbans.

Furthermore, its unique identity is represented by its national symbols, such as the Water Lily as the national flower and the Royal Bengal Tiger as the national animal. Known for its rich cultural heritage and religious harmony, I am deeply proud of my beautiful motherland.
